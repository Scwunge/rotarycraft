# RotaryCraft

Realistic mechanical power. Engines make torque and angular speed; shafts, gearboxes, flywheels, clutches and belts carry it; machines need a minimum of each. Power is torque times speed and
it is conserved, so you gear down for torque and up for speed, and nothing is free.

A port of Reika Kalseki's RotaryCraft to Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge), made with Reika's permission. It needs no other mod.

## What you get

- Eleven kinds of engine, an electric motor and generator, a dynamo
- Shafts and flywheels in five materials, gearboxes in six, clutches, junctions, bevel gears, power buses, belts and chains
- The original's processing machines: grinder, extractor, blast furnace, compactor, centrifuge, fractionation unit, rock melter and many more
- Fluids and pipes: ethanol, jet fuel, lubricant, liquid nitrogen, pumps, reservoirs, gas tanks
- Farming and automation machines, a sorting machine, item filter and a scale-able chest of up to 972 slots
- Lights, a light bridge, a music box, a firework machine, a pile driver, cannons, guns and turrets
- Steel and bedrock tools and armour
- The original's models, textures, screens and sounds, and its recipes, built from its components

## Works with

- JEI: recipe pages for the processing machines, and a description of every machine
- Jade: torque, speed, power, tanks and progress on the machine you look at

## Settings

Everything is a server setting. Machines that change blocks or burn things (the pile driver, line builder, block filler, spiller, spillway, self destruct, block cannon, firestarter) are off until
you switch them on, and they ask claim mods before they build or break, as their owner.
