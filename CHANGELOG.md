# Changelog

## 0.1.0

The first release: a port of Reika Kalseki's RotaryCraft to Minecraft 1.21.1 on NeoForge.

- Engines, shafts, flywheels, gearboxes, clutches, junctions, bevel gears, power buses, belts, chains and portal shafts, with the original's torque, speed and power rules.
- The processing machines: grinder, extractor, blast furnace, friction heater, compactor, centrifuge, fractionation unit, rock melter, pulse furnace, fermenter, crystallizer,
  dryer, composter, refrigerator, boiler, steam turbine, distiller and fuel enhancer; the worktable and auto-crafter.
- Fluids and pipes: ethanol, jet fuel, lubricant, liquid nitrogen, pipes, valves, pumps, reservoirs, gas tanks.
- The farming and automation machines, the world and decoration machines (lights, light bridge, music box, firework machine, pile driver, cannons and others), and the logistics machines
  (sorting machine, item filter, scale-able chest, bucket filler, filling station, wetter, aggregator, spillway, purifier, grindstone, drop processor, item refresher, heaters,
  detectors).
- Weapons, steel and bedrock tools and armour, survey and signal machines.
- Recipes in the original's components; the original's models, textures, screens and sounds.
- A switch for each machine that changes blocks or burns things, off by default; claim-aware block changes.
- Recipe pages and machine descriptions in JEI, torque, speed, power, tanks and progress in Jade.
